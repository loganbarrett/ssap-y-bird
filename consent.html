Online Consent Form: SSAP-y Bird

You are invited to take part in a research study run by the Auditory Perception & Action Lab, Department of Research, Niagara Falls Memorial Medical Center. The person in charge of the study is Logan Barrett.

We are interested in how many people can sing accurately and what kind of problems people have with singing. If you agree to go on, you will take a short singing test that asks you to echo pitches and pitch patterns as well as sing a song from memory. Then there is a short test about hearing pitch changes. At the end you will get your score for the different tests. You must be 18 years old or older to take part.

Your participation does not involve any risk to you beyond that of everyday life. The possible benefits include finding out how well you sing on pitch or where you may need to work on your singing. This study may help researchers to better understand how many people sing on pitch, what kinds of singing problems are more common and what kinds of musical experiences help people sing better. There is no payment.

What is recorded. The game records your singing and saves your test results. We do not ask for your name or email. Your recordings and results are labelled with a participant ID number instead of your name. Because a recording of someone's voice can sometimes identify them, we treat the recordings as private: only the research team can access them, and they are never shared publicly. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps ordinary technical logs such as IP addresses for security; these logs are not part of the research data and the researchers do not see them.

Where your information is kept. Your recordings and results are stored in a password-protected folder belonging to the lab on Google Drive and on a password-protected computer, and are kept for 10 years. Results may appear in scientific papers and presentations, always as group results; your recordings will not be published.

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page. The game shows your participant ID; if you would like your data removed, send that ID to the contact below. Without the ID we cannot find your files.

Questions. If you have questions about this study, contact Logan Barrett (logan.barrett@nfmmc.org). If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, contact the Niagara Falls Memorial Medical Center IRB office at irb@nfmmc.org. The IRB study number is IRB20261103.

Agreeing. If you tick the box and press Accept and continue on the game page, you are confirming that you are at least 18 years old, that you have read this form, and that you agree to take part, including having your singing recorded. If you do not wish to take part, press Decline; you can then still play without sharing any data, and nothing is recorded or sent. If you want a copy of this form, print this page.

You can print this page (Ctrl/Cmd + P) to keep a copy. Close this tab to return to the game.
