Online Consent Form

We are interested in how many people can sing accurately and what kind of problems people have with singing. If you agree to go on, you will take a short singing test that asks you to echo pitches and pitch patterns as well as sing a song from memory. Then there is a short test about hearing pitch changes and a very brief survey on your music training and background. At the end you will get your score for the different tests. We recommend that children under the age of 8 have an adult help them with the measure.

Your participation does not involve any risk to you beyond that of everyday life. The possible benefits include finding out how well you sing on pitch or where you may need to work on your singing. This study may help researchers to better understand how many people sing on pitch, what kinds of singing problems are more common and what kinds of musical experiences help people sing better.

The singing measure is completely anonymous. We will not retain any information that could identify you. All recordings of your singing and your survey responses will be given a new ID number that does not link back to you. We will keep your recordings and survey responses until the database is no longer needed.

If you have any questions about this study, Steven Demorest is the person in charge of this research study and can be reached at 847-467-1682. Questions about your rights as a research subject may be directed to the Institutional Review Board (IRB) Office of Northwestern University at (312) 503-9338.

If you want a copy of this consent for your records, you can print it from the screen. If you would you like documentation linking you to this research study, please email your request to the Principal Investigator at sdemorest@northwestern.edu.
