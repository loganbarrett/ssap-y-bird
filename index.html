About, privacy and credits

What is this? A game version of the Seattle Singing Accuracy Protocol (SSAP), a set of short tests of how accurately people sing, developed by Peter Q. Pfordresher, Steven M. Demorest and colleagues. This version was made for The Auditory Perception & Action Lab at the University at Buffalo.

Privacy. Microphone audio is converted to a pitch number on your device and then discarded. Your results stay on this page; you can download them as a CSV file yourself. Your calibrated voice range, settings and best scores are kept in this browser only.

Accuracy. A note counts as accurate when it is within 50 cents (half a semitone) of the target, the SSAP criterion. This game is for practice and fun; it is not a clinical or diagnostic test.

Method credits. YIN: de Cheveigné & Kawahara (2002), Journal of the Acoustical Society of America 111(4).
